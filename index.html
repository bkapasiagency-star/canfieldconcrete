Why who pours it matters

Bad concrete looks great on day one.

Every slab looks perfect the afternoon it's poured. The shortcuts show up later: after a few winters, a few hundred freeze and thaw cycles and a truck in the driveway. Drag the slider and watch two pours age.

The low-bid pour CHEAPEST QUOTE

  • Poured on dirt, no compacted base
  • Thin slab, no steel
  • Extra water added so it finishes faster
  • No control joints, so it cracks where it wants

A Canfield pour DONE RIGHT

  • Graded and compacted gravel base
  • Full thickness with steel where it counts
  • The right mix for freeze and thaw
  • Joints placed so it moves where we planned

Prep is the whole job

Old concrete out. Ground graded and compacted. Forms set to line and grade, steel in. This is the part nobody sees and the part that decides if it cracks.
